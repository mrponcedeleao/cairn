# Cairn user guide

**This manual is being written.** It is linked from inside the app's guide cards, so the address is here from the start and will not move once the manual lands.

Until then, three things answer most of it:

- **The guides in your deck.** Every Cairn deck opens with six guide cards: one for the app and one for each section. They are the manual in card form, and they are read aloud like any other card. If you have no deck yet, the [starter deck](../packs/) is those six cards and nothing else.
- **The example pack.** Forty cards for a made-up field, in [`packs/`](../packs/), showing what a filled deck looks like before you fill your own.
- **The skills**, in [`skills/`](../skills/), if you want an AI assistant to draft cards with you. Each one says what a good card of its kind is, and ships a check to run before anything enters your deck.

If something in the app confused you, that is exactly what this manual needs to hear about before it is finished: the Contact button in the app, or an issue on this repository.
